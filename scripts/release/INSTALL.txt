KoboAnki - Anki for your Kobo (KOReader plugin)
===============================================

You need: a Kobo with KOReader installed, an AnkiWeb account, Wi-Fi.
Full guide: https://github.com/aangelo861/KoboAnki#install-about-5-minutes

1. Connect the Kobo with USB and tap "Connect" on the Kobo (it shows up as KOBOeReader).
2. Copy the folder "kanki.koplugin" into  KOBOeReader/.adds/koreader/plugins/
   (.adds is hidden: Mac Finder Cmd+Shift+.  /  Windows: View > Hidden items)
   Updating? Replace the old kanki.koplugin folder. Your cards (in .adds/kanki) are kept.
3. Optional, if you use NickelMenu: copy NickelMenu/kanki into  KOBOeReader/.adds/nm/
4. Eject the Kobo. Open KOReader, then the top menu > Anki > Log in.

Mac shortcut for steps 2-4, in Terminal:   bash <this folder>/install-macos.sh
